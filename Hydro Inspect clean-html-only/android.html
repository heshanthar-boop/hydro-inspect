Install in three steps

  1. Download the APK on your Android phone and open the downloaded file.
  2. If Android asks, allow your browser to install this app, then tap Install.
  3. Open HydroInspect, choose your operator name, and enter the six cumulative meter readings.

Check the unit beside each field: CEB and LT use kWh; generator and transformer panels use MWh. Review the values, then submit.

No connection? Keep working.

Fields save on your phone as you type. Submitted readings are locked and queued for upload. Android retries when internet becomes available, including in the background.

Battery restrictions or force-stopping the app can delay sync. Open the Sync tab to check confirmations. Keep automatic phone date/time enabled. Do not uninstall or clear app data before queued readings have synced.

Your existing records stay connected

The app uses the same shared plant database as this website. Only the owner’s verified Google account can edit or delete submitted records. The app opens the secure website for owner editing, inspections, and reports.

Phone entry time and server receipt time are retained separately. Operator names are self-selected; the installation ID and phone model help trace entries.
