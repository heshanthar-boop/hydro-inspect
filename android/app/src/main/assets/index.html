How it works

Drafts stay on this phone. Submit a complete round to queue it for upload. Your submitted entry is locked immediately.

Android retries when internet returns, including in the background. Battery restrictions may delay uploads. Reopen this app to check the queue.

The reading time comes from this phone’s clock (Sri Lanka time). The server also records when it receives the upload. Keep automatic date and time enabled.

Operator names are self-selected. Installation ID and phone model help trace entries; they do not verify a person’s identity.

Do not uninstall or clear app data while readings are waiting to upload. This removes the local queue.
